# Schedule A, as implemented

Schedule A — Sales Guidelines Commission Schedule, effective January 1, 2025
(version 01/01/2025), transcribed, plus every interpretation the engine makes
where the printed schedule leaves room for one.

Read this alongside `assets/rules.js`. If Corporate's number ever disagrees
with the app's, the answer is almost certainly one of the judgement calls
recorded at the bottom.

### Cemetery commission exceptions

- No commissions or volume on AN Interment Authorization Fees (O/Cs). PN
  Interment Authorization Fees (O/Cs) have a commission rate of 5%.
- Does not include unlined vaults. Unlined vaults are paid at 0% commission.
- Undesigned merchandise pays the same percentages listed, less 5%, to a
  minimum of 0%.
- No contracts may be written for longer than 60 months.
- Maximum 10% commission rate on large sales over $25,000. The actual
  percentage is determined by Corporate.

## Judgement calls

Places where the printed schedule is silent or ambiguous, and what the engine
does. Each one is a candidate explanation if a number ever disagrees with
Corporate's.

1. **"N/A" in an ACH column** means the distinction does not apply to that row
   — an at-need or paid-in-full sale has no draft. The engine uses the
   without-ACH percentage and says so.

2. **10 – 19% down with terms of 24 months or less.** The schedule's row 3
   covers 20%+ down up to 24 months and 12%+ down up to 12 months; rows 4 and
   5 are term-length based. A contract at, say, 15% down over 24 months
   matches no row exactly. It is rated at the 25 – 36 month row — which is
   moot in practice, because rows 3 and 4 carry identical percentages.

3. **Terms over 60 months** are not allowed. The engine still produces a
   number (at the 37 – 60 month row) so the contract isn't invisible, but
   flags it loudly.

4. **The large sale cap applies before the company lead take-away.** The cap
   is a maximum commission rate; the take-away only reduces further.

5. **Fixed-rate items are not adjusted.** O&C fees (5% PN / 0% AN) and unlined
   vaults (0%) come from the exceptions block, not the rate table, so the
   large sale cap and the company lead take-away do not touch them.

6. **Unlined vaults count toward bonus volume.** The exception says they are
   paid at 0% *commission*; only AN O&C fees are called out as earning no
   volume.

7. **The company lead take-away is applied once per contract**, per the note,
   no matter how many triggers apply.

8. **Splits reduce your commission, not the bonus volume**, matching how the
   company sheet keys its volume columns at the full contract amount. A
   per-sale toggle under Advanced splits the volume too when a contract calls
   for it.

9. **Bonus tier gaps.** The printed tiers leave gaps ($29,999 to $30,000;
   $44,999 to $45,000). The engine treats each tier as "at or above" its lower
   bound, so $29,999.50 pays the 1% tier.

10. **Sales years other than 2025** are projected from the published
    calendar's 5-4-4 week pattern, marked `(est.)`, and carry no bonus pay
    date. The real calendar shifts days around holidays and must be replaced
    with the published one when it is issued.

11. **A PN trust that turns AN within 30 days** is charged back only when the
    earned rate is higher than the 4% / 2% floor — the schedule says "reduced
    (if higher)", so the chargeback never raises a rate.
